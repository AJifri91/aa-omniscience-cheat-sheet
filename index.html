AA-Omniscience
Benchmark Cheat Sheet

Reliability, not just recall. The benchmark uses 6,000 expert-level factual questions with no tools or supplied context. Correct answers gain a point, hallucinations lose a point, and honest abstentions score zero.

Accuracy (Acc)Share of all questions answered correctly: Accuracy (%) = c. Rewards correct answers but does not punish guessing.
Hallucination Rate (HR)Bluffing on misses: 100 × i / (i + p + a). Lower is better.
Omniscience Index (OI)Reliability: c − i, from −100 to +100. Standard conversion: (OI ÷ 2) + 50.

Legend · How to read the table

One row per model: the highest labeled reasoning level available in the collected data. Selection follows max → xhigh → high → medium → low → minimal, regardless of benchmark score. Different model versions remain separate.

c · Correct
Percentage of all questions answered fully correctly.
i · Incorrect
Percentage of all questions graded incorrect (hallucinated answers).
p · Partial
Partially correct or insufficiently specific answers.
a · Abstain
Questions not attempted, such as an explicit “I don’t know.”
p+a · Partial + abstain
Combined percentage of all questions in these two categories. The collected metrics do not separate them. c + i + p+a ≈ 100%.
Acc · Accuracy
The correct-answer percentage, equal to c. Higher is better.
OI · Omniscience Index
c − i, from −100 to +100. Higher is better; zero means equal correct and incorrect shares.
HR · Hallucination Rate
100 × i ÷ (i + p + a). The percentage of non-correct responses that are incorrect. Lower is better.
Standard % · Rescaled OI
(OI ÷ 2) + 50 maps the Index to 0–100. This is a rescaled score, not accuracy or a probability of correctness.
Profile
An automatic summary based on metric thresholds, written for this cheat sheet; not an official Artificial Analysis rating.

c, i and p+a are percentages, not counts out of 6,000. Derived values and rounding may cause small differences.
