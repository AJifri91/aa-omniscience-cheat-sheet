AA-Omniscience
Benchmark Cheat Sheet

Reliability, not just recall. On 6,000 expert-level questions without tools or context, correct answers earn +1, incorrect answers −1, and partial answers or abstentions 0.

Legend · How to read the table

c, i, p and a are percentages of all questions.

c · Correct (Accuracy)
Fully correct answers. Higher is better.
i · Incorrect
Answers that contradict or differ in meaning from the reference answer.
p · Partial
Accurate, nearly complete answers that lack the required detail or precision.
a · Abstain
No answer attempted, including “I don’t know.”
p+a
Partial answers plus abstentions; both score 0 in OI. c + i + p + a = 100% before rounding.
OI · Omniscience Index
c − i using the percentages above. Higher is better; range −100 to +100. 0 means equal correct and incorrect shares.
HR · Hallucination Rate
100 × i ÷ (i + p + a): incorrect share of all non-correct responses. Lower is better.
Standard %
(OI ÷ 2) + 50. Custom 0–100 rescaling of OI; not accuracy or an official AA metric.
Profile
Our summary of the metrics, not an official AA rating.
