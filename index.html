AA-Omniscience
Benchmark Cheat Sheet

Reliability, not just recall. On 6,000 expert-level questions without tools or context, correct answers earn +1, incorrect answers −1, and abstentions 0.

Legend · How to read the table

c · Correct (Accuracy)
Correct answers as a share of all questions. Higher is better.
i · Incorrect
Incorrect answers as a share of all questions.
p · Partial
Partially correct answers.
a · Abstain
Unanswered questions, including “I don’t know.”
p+a
Partial answers plus abstentions. c + i + p+a ≈ 100%.
OI · Omniscience Index
c − i. Higher is better; range −100 to +100.
HR · Hallucination Rate
100 × i ÷ (i + p + a). Lower is better.
Standard %
(OI ÷ 2) + 50. A rescaled OI, not accuracy.
Profile
Our summary of the metrics, not an official AA rating.
